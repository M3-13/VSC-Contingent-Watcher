# Read the status bar

The status bar item shows your Copilot quota. What it displays depends on the `contingentWatcher.statusBar.display` setting: remaining, used, percent or daily budget.

Hover for a tooltip with:

- usage and how much of the billing month has passed
- remaining quota and your **daily budget**, the amount you can use per day and still reach the reset
- your current pace and, if you are too fast, the day the quota runs out
- the reset date and a saving tip

Click the item for a details menu with quick actions.

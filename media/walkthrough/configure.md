# Make it yours

Open the settings with **Copilot Contingent: Open Settings**. The most useful ones:

- **Display mode** (`statusBar.display`): remaining, used, percent or daily budget.
- **Warning thresholds** (`warnings.thresholds`): usage percentages that trigger a notification, each once per cycle.
- **Pace warning** (`warnings.paceWarning`): warns when your current pace will not last until the reset.
- **Weekends** (`budget.countWeekends`): off by default, so the daily budget is spread over workdays only.
- **Reserve** (`budget.reserve`): hold back a part of the quota for emergencies; it is excluded from the daily budget.
- **Refresh interval** (`refreshIntervalMinutes`): how often the quota is fetched.

Too many notifications? Run **Snooze Warnings for 24 Hours**.
